PNG vs JPG

PNG supports transparent backgrounds and lossless image encoding. JPG uses lossy compression and does not support transparency. JPG can be useful for photographs, but file sizes depend on the original image and chosen quality.

Private browser-based processing

This tool uses the Canvas API to decode PNG images, draw the chosen background, and export JPG files locally. Selected image contents are not sent to FreeAITools servers for conversion.

Important limitations

JPG export removes transparency and may introduce compression artifacts. EXIF, GPS, color profile and other metadata may not be retained. Image dimensions are preserved within the supported limits. Animated PNG is not supported.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PNG to JPG free?

Yes. The converter is free and does not require an account.

Can I convert multiple PNG files?

Yes. Convert up to 15 images at once and download the results as a ZIP.

What happens to transparent PNG backgrounds?

Transparent pixels are replaced by the background color you choose before exporting JPG.

Can I choose a black background?

Yes. Use the background color picker and select black or any other supported color.

Can I control JPG quality?

Yes. Select a quality value from 40% to 95%. File sizes and results can vary by browser.

Will JPG always be smaller than PNG?

No. Some images may become larger, especially at high JPG quality.

Will the image dimensions change?

No. The tool preserves the decoded pixel width and height within its limits.

Are animated PNG files supported?

No. This version rejects APNG animation instead of silently converting only one frame.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Conversion happens locally in your browser.
