Brightness, contrast and saturation

Brightness changes how light or dark an image appears. Contrast changes the difference between light and dark tones. Saturation changes how strong or muted colors appear.

Free grayscale image converter

Turn colorful pictures into grayscale without uploading them to a server. You can also combine grayscale with brightness and contrast adjustments.

Private image editing

Images are decoded and edited using the browser Canvas API. No paid API key, account or external image-processing library is required.

Important limitations

This tool supports static JPG, PNG and WebP images. Animated formats are not supported. PNG and supported WebP exports can preserve transparency, while JPG replaces transparent areas with white.

Images are processed using standard 8-bit RGB color values. Different browsers and color profiles may produce slightly different results. Original EXIF, GPS and other metadata may not be preserved.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Image Grayscale & Filters free?

Yes. The tool is free and needs no account.

Can I make an image black and white?

Yes. Enable Grayscale or select the Black & White preset.

Can I adjust brightness?

Yes. Move the Brightness slider to make the image lighter or darker.

What does Contrast change?

Contrast changes the separation between lighter and darker pixel values.

Can I remove all color?

Yes. Set Saturation to 0% or enable Grayscale.

Can I combine filters?

Yes. Brightness, Contrast, Saturation and Grayscale can be used together.

Will image dimensions change?

No. The downloaded image keeps the decoded width and height within supported limits.

Can I download PNG, JPG or WebP?

Yes, if your browser supports encoding the selected output format.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Image editing happens locally in your browser.
