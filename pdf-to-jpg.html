Images are generated one page at a time. One selected page downloads as JPG. Multiple pages download together as ZIP. Each output page is limited to 12 million pixels and 10,000 pixels per side.

How to convert PDF to JPG

  1. Choose a PDF file from your device.
  2. Enter "all" or select specific page numbers.
  3. Choose the image resolution and JPG quality.
  4. Click Convert PDF to JPG.
  5. Download a JPG file or a ZIP containing the images.

Private PDF processing

The selected PDF is processed locally inside your browser using PDF.js. PDF contents are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers for conversion.

An internet connection is needed to load the PDF processing libraries. Encrypted or password-protected PDF files are not supported in this version.

JPG images do not retain searchable text, interactive PDF elements or vector quality. Some PDFs may render differently depending on embedded fonts and advanced features.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PDF to JPG free?

Yes. You can convert supported PDF pages to JPG for free without creating an account.

Can I convert every page?

Yes, if your PDF has 20 pages or fewer. For longer PDFs, select up to 20 pages per conversion.

Can I choose specific PDF pages?

Yes. Enter page numbers or ranges such as 1,3-5.

How are multiple JPG files downloaded?

If you select multiple pages, the converter places the JPG images in one ZIP file.

Are PDF files uploaded?

No. The selected PDF contents are converted locally in your browser, not uploaded to FreeAITools servers.

Will PDF text remain selectable?

No. JPG is an image format, so text becomes pixels and cannot be selected as PDF text.

Can I convert password-protected PDFs?

No. This version does not support encrypted or password-protected PDF documents.

What if my PDF is very large?

Large PDF pages may require significant memory. Try a lower resolution or select fewer pages.
