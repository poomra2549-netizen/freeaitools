    How to convert JPG images to PDF

    1. Choose one or more JPG or PNG images.
    2. Use the arrow buttons to change their order.
    3. Select A4 or US Letter page size.
    4. Click Convert to PDF.
    5. Download your PDF and review the pages.

    Private browser-based processing

    Your images are processed locally in your browser using the pdf-lib JavaScript library. Selected image contents are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers for conversion.

    An internet connection is needed to load the PDF processing library.

    Each image becomes a separate PDF page. The original image bytes are embedded without intentional JPEG recompression. Some metadata and EXIF orientation information may not be preserved in the output.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is JPG to PDF free?

    Yes. This converter is free and does not require an account.

    Can I combine several images into one PDF?

    Yes. Select up to 20 supported images and arrange them before converting. Each image becomes one page.

    Can I convert PNG to PDF?

    Yes. JPG and PNG files are supported. Both can be included in the same PDF.

    Will images be uploaded?

    No. Selected images are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers for conversion.

    Can I choose the page size?

    Yes. Choose A4 or US Letter. Images are scaled proportionally to fit the page.

    What happens to transparent PNG images?

    Transparent areas appear against the white PDF page background.

    Why might conversion fail?

    A file may be damaged, too large or unsupported. Very large documents may also exceed the memory available on your device.

    Can I change the image order?

    Yes. Use the up and down arrow buttons before creating your PDF.
