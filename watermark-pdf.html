How to add a watermark to PDF

  1. Select a PDF file from your device.
  2. Enter your watermark text.
  3. Choose a position, size, color and opacity.
  4. Set the rotation and select the target pages.
  5. Click Add Watermark and Save PDF.
  6. Download and review the updated PDF.

Private PDF processing

The selected PDF is processed locally in your browser using the pdf-lib JavaScript library. PDF contents are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers for watermarking.

An internet connection is required to load the PDF processing library.

Important limitations

Watermarks are added as PDF page content. They are not tamper-proof security protection and may be modified or removed using specialized PDF software.

Advanced PDF features such as digital signatures, forms, annotations and interactive elements may not be preserved exactly after saving. Always check the downloaded document.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Watermark PDF free?

Yes. This tool is free and does not require an account.

Can I watermark every page?

Yes. Enter "all" to apply the watermark to every page in a supported PDF.

Can I watermark only selected pages?

Yes. Enter page numbers such as 1,3-5.

Can I make the watermark transparent?

Yes. Use the opacity slider to adjust its visibility.

Can I change watermark position?

Yes. Choose the center or one of the four corners. You can also select horizontal or diagonal text.

Can I use Thai text?

Not in this version. The tool currently supports basic English and ASCII characters only.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

No. Selected PDF contents are processed locally in your browser.

Does the watermark permanently protect my PDF?

No. A watermark is a visual marking, not an encryption or document security feature.
