ABOUT US

Practical AI for everyday tasks.

FreeAITools is an independent project designed to make common writing tasks easier. Our tools help people create first drafts, explore ideas and communicate more clearly.

Our approach

We aim to keep our tools simple, explain their limitations and encourage users to review AI-generated content before publishing or relying on it. AI can make mistakes, so please verify important details.

We are continuously improving the site based on usefulness, clarity and responsible use.
