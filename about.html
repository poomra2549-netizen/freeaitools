ABOUT FREEAITOOLS

Simple tools.
More productive work.

FreeAITools is an independent project focused on making everyday digital tasks simpler. Our free browser-based utilities help you work with PDF documents and images without complicated software or account registration.

What is FreeAITools?

FreeAITools brings practical online utilities together in one place. The goal is to provide straightforward tools with clear instructions, useful results and accessible interfaces.

We focus on common tasks such as combining PDF documents, extracting pages, rotating files, converting image formats, resizing pictures and reducing image file sizes.

Our approach

We aim to make tools easy to understand, with practical guidance and honest explanations of their capabilities.

Our PDF and image tools are designed to process selected files locally in your browser rather than uploading file contents to FreeAITools servers for processing. Your browser still connects to the website and may load supporting software.

We recommend keeping backups of important files and checking downloaded results before relying on them.

About our AI features

FreeAITools also includes interfaces for AI writing, social media captions, business name ideas and email drafts.
