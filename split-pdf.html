Split PDF Files

Extract selected pages from your PDF. Free, simple, and private.

Enter individual page numbers or ranges separated by commas. Example: 1-3,5,7-9.

Maximum file size: 50 MB. Large PDFs may require more device memory.

How to split a PDF

  1. Select a PDF file from your device.
  2. Enter the pages you want to extract.
  3. Click Split PDF.
  4. Download your new PDF document.

Examples

1-3 extracts pages 1, 2 and 3.

2,5,7 extracts pages 2, 5 and 7.

1-3,6-8 extracts two page ranges.

Private PDF processing

Your documents are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to our servers. The PDF library is loaded from a CDN.

Password-protected PDFs are not supported. This tool extracts selected pages into one PDF.
