Split PDF Files

Extract selected pages from your PDF. Free, simple, and private.

Enter individual page numbers or ranges separated by commas. Example: 1-3,5,7-9.

Maximum file size: 50 MB. Large PDFs may require more device memory.

How to split a PDF

  1. Select a PDF file from your device.
  2. Enter the pages you want to extract.
  3. Click Split PDF.
  4. Download your new PDF document.

Examples

1-3 extracts pages 1, 2 and 3.

2,5,7 extracts pages 2, 5 and 7.

1-3,6-8 extracts two page ranges.

Private PDF processing

Your documents are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to our servers. The PDF library is loaded from a CDN.

Password-protected PDFs are not supported. This tool extracts selected pages into one PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF splitter free?

Yes. You can extract pages from PDF files for free without creating an account.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. Your selected PDF is processed locally in your browser. The document is not uploaded to our servers for splitting.

How do I select specific pages?

Enter page numbers separated by commas. For example, 2,5,7 extracts pages 2, 5 and 7. You can also use ranges such as 1-3,6-8.

Can I extract multiple page ranges?

Yes. You can combine several ranges in one request, such as 1-3,5,7-9. All selected pages are saved into one new PDF document.

What is the maximum PDF file size?

The maximum supported file size is 50 MB. Large PDFs may require additional browser memory.

Can I split password-protected PDFs?

No. Password-protected PDFs are not supported by this tool.
