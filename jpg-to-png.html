Why convert JPG to PNG?

PNG is widely supported in image editing and graphic design software. Unlike JPG, PNG uses lossless encoding when saving image pixels. However, converting JPG to PNG cannot recover image details already lost through JPG compression.

Private browser-based conversion

This tool decodes JPG/JPEG images and exports PNG files using your browser's Canvas API. No image contents are uploaded to FreeAITools servers for conversion.

Important limitations

PNG output may be significantly larger than the original JPG. Image width and height are preserved within the tool limits, but EXIF, GPS metadata, color profiles and other original metadata may not be retained.

Converting JPG to PNG does not automatically create a transparent background. The resulting PNG normally remains opaque.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is JPG to PNG free?

Yes. This converter is free and does not require an account.

Can I convert multiple JPG images?

Yes. You can convert up to 15 images at once and download all results as ZIP.

Are .jpg and .jpeg both supported?

Yes. Both common JPEG file extensions are supported.

Will PNG make my image higher quality?

No. PNG cannot restore details that were already lost in the original JPG.

Will PNG files be smaller?

Not necessarily. PNG images are often larger than JPG images.

Will my PNG have a transparent background?

No. Converting a JPG to PNG does not remove its existing background.

Can I change PNG quality?

PNG export in this tool is lossless, so there is no JPG-style quality slider.

Will image dimensions change?

No. The converter keeps the decoded pixel dimensions within its limits.

Are images uploaded?

No. Images are processed locally in your browser.
