How to remove pages from PDF

  1. Choose a PDF file from your device.
  2. Enter the page numbers you want to delete.
  3. Review the highlighted page numbers.
  4. Click Remove Pages and Save PDF.
  5. Download and check the updated document.

Example

For a PDF containing 10 pages, entering 2,4-6 removes pages 2, 4, 5 and 6.

The resulting PDF contains original pages 1, 3, 7, 8, 9 and 10, in that order.

Private browser-based editing

This tool processes the selected PDF locally inside your browser using pdf-lib. File contents are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers for editing.

Internet access is required to load the PDF processing library.

Some advanced PDF elements, such as forms, bookmarks, annotations and digital signatures, may be affected when saving. Always review the downloaded result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Remove PDF Pages free?

Yes. This tool is free and does not require account registration.

Can I delete several PDF pages at once?

Yes. Enter a comma-separated list such as 2,5,8 or use ranges such as 3-7.

Can I remove the first or last page?

Yes. Any page can be removed, provided at least one page remains in the resulting PDF.

Can I delete every page?

No. The tool prevents removing every page because the downloaded PDF must contain at least one page.

Will the original PDF be changed?

No. Your original file remains unchanged. The tool creates a separate PDF for download.

Are PDF documents uploaded?

No. PDF processing happens locally in your browser for this operation.

Does removing pages compress the PDF?

Not necessarily. Removing pages can reduce file size, but it is not a dedicated PDF compression process.

What are the limits?

Input PDF files must be no larger than 40 MB and must contain no more than 200 pages. Encrypted or password-protected PDFs are not supported.
