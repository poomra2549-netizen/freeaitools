How to crop an image

  1. Upload a JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Choose an aspect ratio or use Free mode.
  3. Drag to select the area you want to keep.
  4. Move the frame to adjust its position.
  5. Choose PNG or JPG output.
  6. Preview the crop and download your image.

Popular aspect ratios

A 1:1 crop creates a square image. A 4:3 crop is suitable for traditional landscape layouts. A 16:9 crop creates a widescreen image, while 9:16 is useful for vertical layouts.

Private image cropping

This tool uses the Canvas API inside your browser. The crop operation does not send your image to FreeAITools servers. No API key or account is required.

Important limitations

This version supports static JPG, PNG and WebP images. Animated images, including APNG and animated WebP, are not supported.

PNG can preserve transparency, while JPG cannot. Transparent pixels are replaced with white when exporting JPG. Original EXIF, GPS and other metadata may not be preserved. Image orientation can depend on the browser's decoding.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Image Cropper free?

Yes. You can crop supported images without paying or creating an account.

Can I crop a square image?

Yes. Choose the Square 1:1 aspect ratio.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The selection supports mouse, pen and touch input through browser pointer events.

Can I move the selected area?

Yes. Drag inside the green frame or use the arrow buttons for small adjustments.

Can I export JPG?

Yes. Select JPG as the output format and choose the quality.

Will PNG transparency remain?

PNG output can retain transparent pixels inside the cropped area.

Will cropping reduce file size?

Sometimes. File size depends on the cropped area, output format and compression.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Cropping is performed locally in your browser.
