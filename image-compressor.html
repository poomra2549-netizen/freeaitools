How to compress an image

  1. Select a JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Choose WebP or JPG as the output format.
  3. Adjust the image quality slider.
  4. Click Compress Image.
  5. Compare file sizes and download the result.

What format should I choose?

WebP: Often provides efficient compression and supports transparency.

JPG: Widely compatible and suitable for photographs, but does not support transparency.

PNG input: You can reduce its size by converting it to WebP or JPG. This changes the image format.

Private processing

Your image is processed inside your browser with the Canvas API. Image contents are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers.

Compression does not guarantee a smaller result. Some image metadata may be removed. Animated images are not supported.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image compressor free?

Yes. You can compress images for free without creating an account.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image processing happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your selected images are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers for compression.

Which image formats can I compress?

You can select JPG, PNG and WebP files. The output can be WebP or JPG, depending on your browser's capabilities.

Which format should I choose?

WebP is often a good choice for smaller files and supports transparency. JPG is widely compatible and works well for photographs.

What does the quality slider do?

It controls the requested output quality for JPG or WebP encoding. Lower settings often reduce file size but can also reduce visible image detail.

Will my image always become smaller?

No. The output may be smaller, the same size, or larger than the original. This depends on the source image, selected format, quality and browser.

Does compression change image dimensions?

No. This tool keeps the original image width and height. To change dimensions, use our Image Resizer.

Will transparent areas be preserved?

WebP supports transparency. JPG does not, so transparent areas are replaced with white when JPG is selected.

What are the file size limits?

Input files must not exceed 25 MB. Image dimensions must not exceed 10,000 pixels per side or 40 million pixels in total.

Are animated images supported?

No. This tool is intended for still images. Animation and some original metadata may not be preserved.
