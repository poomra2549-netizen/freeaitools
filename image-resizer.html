How to resize images

  1. Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Enter your desired width or height.
  3. Keep the aspect ratio locked to avoid stretching.
  4. Choose an output format.
  5. Click Resize Image and download the result.

Private image processing

Your image is processed locally using the browser Canvas API. Image contents are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers.

JPG does not support transparency. Transparent areas become white. Some metadata may be removed, and animated images are not supported.
