Private image processing

JPG does not support transparency. Transparent areas become white. Some metadata may be removed, and animated images are not supported.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image resizer free?

Yes. You can resize JPG, PNG and WebP images for free without creating an account.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Images are resized locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Image contents are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers for resizing.

How do I resize an image?

Select an image, enter your desired width or height in pixels, choose an output format, and click Resize Image.

What does Keep aspect ratio mean?

It automatically adjusts the other dimension when you change the width or height, helping preserve the original image proportions.

Can I resize without keeping the aspect ratio?

Yes. Uncheck Keep aspect ratio to set the width and height independently. This may stretch or distort the image.

Which output formats are supported?

You can export JPG, PNG or WebP images, depending on your browser's capabilities. JPG does not support transparency.

What are the image size limits?

Input images must be no larger than 25 MB and 40 million pixels. Output dimensions must not exceed 10,000 pixels per side or 40 million pixels in total.

Will resizing reduce the file size?

Often, but not always. File size depends on the output dimensions, image content and format. Enlarging an image does not create additional original detail.

Are animated images supported?

No. This tool is designed for still images. Animation and some image metadata may not be preserved.
