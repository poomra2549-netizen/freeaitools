Why use WebP?

WebP is a modern image format that can produce smaller files than JPG or PNG in many situations. Smaller image files can help website pages load faster. However, a smaller file is not guaranteed for every input image.

Private browser-based processing

Image conversion uses the Canvas API inside your browser. The selected images are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers for conversion.

Important limitations

This version converts supported static JPG and PNG images. Animated images are not supported. Color profiles, EXIF metadata, GPS metadata and some other original image information may not be preserved. Transparent PNG images can remain transparent in WebP, depending on browser encoding.

The quality slider uses the browser's WebP encoder. Output size and appearance may differ between browsers. Images are not resized in this tool.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Image to WebP free?

Yes. You can convert supported images to WebP for free without creating an account.

Can I convert multiple images at once?

Yes. You can select up to 15 JPG or PNG images and download all converted images as a ZIP.

Can I adjust WebP image quality?

Yes. Choose a quality value from 40% to 95% before converting.

Will WebP always be smaller?

No. The output can be larger or smaller depending on image content and the original encoding.

Does this tool resize images?

No. It preserves the decoded image dimensions, subject to the tool's limits.

Does WebP support transparency?

Yes. WebP supports transparency, and transparent PNG images can retain transparent areas.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Conversion happens locally in your browser.

Are animated images supported?

No. This version is intended for static JPG and PNG images only.

Why does conversion fail in my browser?

Your browser may not support WebP encoding, the file may be damaged, or the image may exceed available device memory.
