Rotate PDF Files

Rotate all pages in your PDF. Free, simple, and private.

The selected rotation will be applied to every page of the document.

Maximum file size: 50 MB. Large files may require more device memory.

How to rotate a PDF

  1. Select a PDF from your device.
  2. Choose the rotation angle.
  3. Click Rotate PDF.
  4. Download your rotated document.

Available rotations

90° clockwise: Turn pages to the right.

180°: Turn pages upside down.

90° counterclockwise: Turn pages to the left.

Your privacy matters

PDF documents are processed locally in your browser. Your documents are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers. An internet connection is needed to load the PDF processing library.

Password-protected PDFs are not supported. This tool rotates every page in the PDF.
