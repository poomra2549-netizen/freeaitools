Rotate PDF Files

Rotate all pages in your PDF. Free, simple, and private.

The selected rotation will be applied to every page of the document.

Maximum file size: 50 MB. Large files may require more device memory.

How to rotate a PDF

  1. Select a PDF from your device.
  2. Choose the rotation angle.
  3. Click Rotate PDF.
  4. Download your rotated document.

Available rotations

90° clockwise: Turn pages to the right.

180°: Turn pages upside down.

90° counterclockwise: Turn pages to the left.

Your privacy matters

PDF documents are processed locally in your browser. Your documents are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers. An internet connection is needed to load the PDF processing library.

Password-protected PDFs are not supported. This tool rotates every page in the PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF rotation tool free?

Yes. You can rotate PDF documents for free without creating an account.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. PDF rotation is performed locally in your browser. Your selected documents are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers.

Which rotation angles are available?

You can rotate PDF pages 90 degrees clockwise, 180 degrees, or 90 degrees counterclockwise.

Can I rotate only one page?

This tool currently rotates every page in the selected PDF. Rotating individual pages is not supported.

Will rotating a PDF remove its pages?

The tool changes the rotation settings of the pages rather than intentionally removing pages from the document.

What is the maximum file size?

The maximum file size is 50 MB. Large documents may require more memory on your device.

Can I rotate a password-protected PDF?

No. Password-protected PDF documents are not supported by this tool.
