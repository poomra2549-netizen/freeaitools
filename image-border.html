What are rounded corners?

Rounded corners soften the rectangular edges of an image. This can be useful for website graphics, presentations, social media content and product photos.

Will a border change image dimensions?

Yes. The border is added outside the original image. For example, a 1000 × 600 image with a 20-pixel border becomes 1040 × 640 pixels.

Private browser-based editing

Image borders and rounded corners are drawn using your browser's Canvas API. No API key, account or external image-editing library is required.

Important limitations

This tool supports static JPG, PNG and WebP images. Animated PNG and animated WebP files are not supported.

PNG and supported WebP exports can preserve transparent rounded corners. JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas become white. The original EXIF, GPS and other metadata may not be retained.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Image Border free?

Yes. The tool is free and needs no account.

Can I change the border color?

Yes. Use the color picker to choose your preferred color.

Can I create rounded corners without a border?

Yes. Set Border thickness to 0 and increase Corner radius.

Does the border cover part of my picture?

No. The border is placed around the original image rather than covering its center.

Will rounded corners be transparent?

Yes, in PNG output and generally in WebP output when supported by your browser.

Why are JPG corners white?

JPG does not support transparent pixels, so empty rounded corners use white.

What does Polaroid-style mean?

It applies a thick white frame and small rounded corners. It does not add a larger bottom margin.

Can I download WebP?

Yes, if your browser supports WebP encoding.

Are my images uploaded?

No. All border editing happens in your browser.
