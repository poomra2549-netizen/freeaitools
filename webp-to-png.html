Why convert WebP to PNG?

PNG is useful for editing workflows, graphics that require transparency, and software that does not accept WebP. PNG is a lossless format, although conversion cannot restore details already lost in a lossy WebP image.

Private browser-based conversion

This tool decodes WebP images and exports PNG files using the browser Canvas API. Selected image contents are not sent to FreeAITools servers for conversion. An internet connection is required to load the ZIP library for batch downloads.

Important limitations

PNG output files may be larger than the original WebP files. This version supports static WebP images only. Animation is not preserved. EXIF, GPS data, color profiles and other metadata may not be retained. The original image dimensions are preserved within the stated limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is WebP to PNG free?

Yes. It is free and requires no account.

Can I convert many images at once?

Yes. Convert up to 15 static WebP images per batch and download all results as a ZIP.

Does PNG preserve transparency?

Yes. Transparent areas in a supported WebP image can remain transparent after conversion to PNG.

Will the converted PNG be smaller?

Not necessarily. PNG files are often larger than WebP files.

Can I choose PNG quality?

No. Standard browser PNG export is lossless and does not offer a JPG-style quality slider.

Are animated WebP files supported?

No. This version rejects animated WebP images instead of exporting only one frame.

Does the tool resize images?

No. It preserves image pixel dimensions, provided they are within the supported limits.

Are images uploaded?

No. Images are converted locally in your browser.

Why might conversion fail?

A file may be damaged, unsupported by your browser, too large, or your device may have insufficient memory.
