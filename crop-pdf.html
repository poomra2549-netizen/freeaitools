Free Crop PDF

Trim visible page margins in your PDF. Free, simple and private.

Maximum 30 MB and 100 pages. Pages with PDF rotation are not supported in this version. Password-protected PDFs are not supported.

Adjust crop margins

Enter the amount to remove from each edge. Units are PDF points (pt). 72 pt is approximately 1 inch. Values apply to each selected page.

Use "all" to crop every page or enter page numbers and ranges such as 1,3-5.

Your cropped PDF is ready!

Download Cropped PDF

Cropping changes the visible page area. It does not securely delete hidden content from the PDF file.

How to crop PDF pages

  1. Choose a PDF document.
  2. Set the top, bottom, left and right margins.
  3. Select all pages or specific page numbers.
  4. Check the preview using the page selector.
  5. Click Crop PDF and Download.
  6. Open the downloaded PDF and review the result.

Private browser-based processing

This tool uses pdf-lib and PDF.js inside your browser. PDF contents are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers for cropping. An internet connection is required to load the PDF libraries.

Important limitations

Cropping adjusts the PDF CropBox. The content outside the selected area can still exist inside the PDF file. Do not use cropping to redact confidential information or permanently erase content.

Complex PDFs containing digital signatures, forms or annotations may behave differently after saving. Always review the output.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Crop PDF free?

Yes. This tool is free to use without creating an account.

Can I crop every page at once?

Yes. Enter "all" to apply the margins to every supported page.

Can I crop only selected pages?

Yes. Enter page numbers and ranges such as 1,3-5.

What does 20 pt mean?

It means 20 PDF points. There are approximately 72 points in one inch.

Can I preview the crop?

Yes. Select a page to display its content and the proposed crop boundaries.

Are PDF files uploaded?

No. PDF processing happens locally in your browser.

Does cropping permanently remove content?

No. Cropping hides content outside the visible page area. It does not securely erase it.

Are rotated or password-protected PDFs supported?

No. This version does not support PDFs containing rotated pages or password-protected documents.
