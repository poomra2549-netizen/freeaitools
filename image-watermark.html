How to add a watermark to an image

  1. Select a JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Enter your watermark text.
  3. Adjust the font, size, color and opacity.
  4. Choose one of the nine watermark positions.
  5. Review the live preview.
  6. Select PNG or JPG and prepare your download.

Why watermark images?

A text watermark can help identify a creator, business or source. It can also discourage casual reuse, but it does not technically prevent image copying or watermark removal.

Private browser-based editing

The selected image is decoded and processed locally with the Canvas API. Image contents are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers for watermarking.

Important limitations

PNG output can preserve transparent areas. JPG output does not support transparency and uses a white background instead. Original metadata including EXIF, GPS and some color-profile information may not be preserved.

This version supports static images only. The available fonts are browser-dependent, and text appearance may vary slightly between browsers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Image Watermark free?

Yes. The tool is free without requiring an account.

Can I choose watermark position?

Yes. Choose one of nine positions including the center and corners.

Can I adjust transparency?

Yes. Use the opacity slider to make the watermark more or less visible.

Can I change text color?

Yes. Use the color picker to choose your preferred watermark color.

Can I use Thai text?

Yes, if your browser has a suitable font available for the characters.

Will image dimensions change?

No. The output keeps the decoded image's full pixel dimensions within the tool limits.

Can I download JPG?

Yes. Select JPG and set the desired quality.

Are images uploaded?

No. Watermarking happens locally in your browser.

Can someone remove my watermark?

Possibly. A watermark is not a guarantee against copying or removal.
