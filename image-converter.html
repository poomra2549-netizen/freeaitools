How to convert images

  1. Choose an image from your device.

Supported image formats

JPG: Ideal for photographs and sharing.

PNG: Supports transparency and lossless output encoding.

WebP: Often offers smaller file sizes.

Private browser processing

Images are converted locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Image contents are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers.

Converting to JPG removes transparency. Transparent areas become white. Some image metadata may not be preserved. Animated images are not supported.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image converter free?

Yes. You can convert images for free without creating an account.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image conversion happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your selected images are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers for conversion.

Which image formats are supported?

You can convert between JPG, PNG and WebP, provided your browser supports the selected output format.

Does converting an image reduce its size?

Not always. The output size depends on the image content, selected format and quality settings. WebP often produces smaller files than JPG or PNG.

Can I convert transparent images to JPG?

Yes, but JPG does not support transparency. Transparent areas are filled with white.

Does the quality slider affect PNG?

No. The quality slider applies to JPG and WebP. It is hidden when PNG is selected as the output format.

What is the maximum image size?

Input files are limited to 25 MB and images must not exceed 40 million pixels.

Are animated images supported?

No. This tool converts still images. Animation and some original image metadata may not be preserved.
