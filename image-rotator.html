How to rotate or flip an image

  1. Select a JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Rotate it 90° left, 90° right, or 180°.
  3. Flip it horizontally or vertically if needed.
  4. Check the live preview.
  5. Select PNG, JPG or WebP output.
  6. Prepare the file and download your result.

Rotate vs flip

Rotating an image changes its orientation. A 90° or 270° rotation swaps the width and height. Flipping mirrors the image horizontally or vertically without changing its dimensions.

Private browser-based editing

All image transformations use the browser Canvas API. Image contents are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers for editing. There is no API key or account requirement.

Important limitations

This tool supports static JPG, PNG and WebP. Animated images are not supported. Original metadata such as EXIF and GPS may not be retained.

PNG and supported WebP exports can preserve transparent backgrounds. JPG output uses a white background because JPG does not support transparency. File sizes and encoding results vary between formats.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Image Rotator free?

Yes. This tool is free and requires no account.

Can I rotate an image 90 degrees?

Yes. Use Rotate Left 90° or Rotate Right 90°.

Can I rotate an image 270 degrees?

Yes. Click Rotate Right 90° three times, or Rotate Left 90° once.

Can I mirror an image?

Yes. Choose Flip Horizontal for a left-right mirror or Flip Vertical for an up-down mirror.

Can I combine rotation and flipping?

Yes. Multiple actions can be combined and previewed.

Can I reset the image?

Yes. Reset Changes restores the original displayed orientation without removing the selected file.

Will a transparent PNG remain transparent?

Yes, when exported to PNG, and generally when exported to WebP in a supporting browser.

Will JPG retain transparency?

No. JPG output fills transparent areas with white.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Image transformations happen locally in your browser.
