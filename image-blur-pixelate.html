How to blur or pixelate an image

  1. Select a JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Choose Blur, Pixelate or Solid Cover.
  3. Drag over the part of the image to edit.
  4. Select a region to change its effect or strength.
  5. Add more regions if needed and inspect the preview.
  6. Choose PNG, JPG or WebP and download.

Blur vs pixelate vs solid cover

Blur softens details. Pixelate converts an area into larger blocks of color. Neither should be trusted to permanently conceal confidential data. Solid Cover paints an opaque rectangle over the selected pixels and is the safer option for that purpose, but you should verify the exported image before sharing.

Private browser-based editing

The image is decoded and transformed with the Canvas API on your device. No account, paid API or external library is required. Output images may not retain EXIF metadata, but you should not rely on this as a guaranteed metadata-removal tool.

Important limitations

Only static JPG, PNG and WebP are supported. Regions are rectangular. Effects near a region's edges may look different from professional desktop photo editors. Existing regions are selected by tapping, not dragged to reposition; remove and redraw a region to move it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Image Blur & Pixelate free?

Yes. The tool is free and requires no account.

Can I blur multiple areas?

Yes. Draw multiple rectangles, each with its own effect and strength.

Can I pixelate a face or text?

Yes, but pixelation is not secure redaction for private information.

How can I hide sensitive information?

Choose Solid Cover, select a fully opaque color, cover the entire sensitive region and verify the downloaded result.

Can I change an existing region?

Yes. Tap a region, then change its effect, intensity or cover color.

Will image dimensions change?

No. The edited image retains the decoded source dimensions.

Can I download JPG and WebP?

Yes, if your browser can encode the selected format. PNG is also available.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Editing is performed inside your browser.
