How to add page numbers to PDF

  1. Select a PDF file.
  2. Choose the page number style and position.
  3. Choose where numbering begins and the first number.
  4. Adjust font size, color and margins.
  5. Click Add Page Numbers and Save PDF.
  6. Download and review the new document.

Private browser-based processing

Selected PDF contents are processed locally in your browser using pdf-lib. They are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers for numbering. An internet connection is needed to load the PDF processing library.

Important limitations

This tool adds visible page numbers; it does not change the document's underlying page order. For advanced PDFs, links, interactive forms and digital signatures may be affected by saving. Page numbers may overlap existing content. Always review the resulting document.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is adding PDF page numbers free?

Yes. The tool is free and does not require account registration.

Can I skip the cover page?

Yes. Set Start numbering on PDF page to 2.

Can I start numbering at a number other than 1?

Yes. Change First page number to the number you want.

Can I put page numbers at the top?

Yes. You can choose top or bottom positions, aligned left, center or right.

Can I show total page count?

Yes. Choose the Page 1 of 10 format. The total counts pages included in the numbered section.

Are PDF files uploaded?

No. Numbering is processed locally in the browser.

Does this remove existing page numbers?

No. The tool adds numbers and does not automatically detect or erase existing numbers.

Can I use a password-protected PDF?

No. This version does not support encrypted or password-protected PDFs.
