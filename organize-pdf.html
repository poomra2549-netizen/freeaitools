Reorder, rotate and remove PDF pages. Free, simple and private.

Maximum file size: 30 MB. Maximum 60 pages. Password-protected PDFs are not supported.

Organize your pages

Use ↑ and ↓ to move pages, ↺ and ↻ to rotate pages by 90°, or Remove to delete a page. Changes are saved only when you download the new PDF.

Download Organized PDF

Page previews are generated inside your browser. Large or complex PDFs may require additional time and device memory.

How to organize PDF pages

  1. Choose a PDF file from your device.
  2. Review the page previews.
  3. Move pages up or down to change their order.
  4. Rotate individual pages or remove unwanted pages.
  5. Click Save Organized PDF and download the result.

Privacy and limitations

PDF contents are processed locally in your browser using PDF.js and pdf-lib. Selected PDF files are not uploaded to FreeAITools servers for editing.

Internet access is needed to load the PDF libraries. This tool does not support password-protected PDFs. Advanced forms, annotations, links, bookmarks or digital signatures may not be preserved exactly. Always review the downloaded PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Organize PDF free?

Yes. You can organize PDF pages for free without creating an account.

Can I change the order of PDF pages?

Yes. Use the up and down buttons under each page preview to rearrange the pages.

Can I delete individual PDF pages?

Yes. Click Remove on any unwanted page. You must keep at least one page.

Can I rotate only one page?

Yes. Each page has its own clockwise and counterclockwise rotation buttons.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

No. The selected PDF is processed locally in your browser for this operation.

Does the original PDF change?

No. The original file remains unchanged. You download a separate organized PDF.

What are the limits?

The current version supports PDFs up to 30 MB and 60 pages.

Will all PDF features be preserved?

Not necessarily. Advanced interactive features and digital signatures may be changed or lost. Check important output files.
