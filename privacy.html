LEGAL & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Welcome to FreeAITools. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you visit our website and use our free AI, PDF, and image tools.

Website: https://freeaitools-5pd.pages.dev/

1. Information you provide

When you use our AI writing tools, the text you enter, your selected tool, and relevant generation preferences are processed to respond to your request.

Please do not enter passwords, payment details, government identification numbers, confidential documents, sensitive personal information, or other information you do not want shared with an external AI provider.

2. AI processing and Google Gemini

FreeAITools uses Google's Gemini API to generate text for tools such as AI Writer, Caption Generator, Business Name Generator, and Email Writer.

AI prompts are sent from our Cloudflare-hosted backend to Google's Gemini API. Google processes the submitted content to generate responses.

Our current Gemini API project uses Google's free service tier. Under Google's terms for unpaid Gemini API services, submitted prompts and generated responses may be used to provide, improve, and develop Google's products and services. Human reviewers may review some content as described in Google's terms.

Please review Google's Gemini API Terms and Google's Privacy Policy for further details.

3. PDF and image tools

Our PDF and image tools are designed to process selected files locally in your web browser.

For these browser-based operations, the files you select are processed on your device rather than being intentionally uploaded to our Gemini API backend.

We recommend avoiding highly sensitive files when using any online service. Browser extensions and third-party software installed on your device may have their own data practices.

4. Security and usage limits

We use Cloudflare services to host and protect FreeAITools, verify legitimate requests, and limit automated or excessive use of our AI tools.

Our backend receives technical request information, including the request IP address. For daily rate limiting, it generates a SHA-256 hash from the date and IP address and uses that hash as part of a temporary usage-counter key in Cloudflare KV.

The rate-limit counter is configured with an expiration time of approximately 24 hours. Our system also maintains website-wide AI usage counters.

Hashing an IP address does not necessarily make the resulting information anonymous. We use this processing for service protection, abuse prevention, and fair resource allocation.

5. Cloudflare Turnstile

We use Cloudflare Turnstile to help distinguish legitimate visitors from automated activity before processing AI requests.

Cloudflare may process technical information associated with security checks, such as browser, device, network, and request information, in accordance with its applicable privacy terms.

Learn more in Cloudflare's Privacy Policy .

6. Hosting and technical information

FreeAITools is hosted using Cloudflare infrastructure. Cloudflare may process network and technical information necessary to deliver website content, maintain service performance, and protect against abuse.

The handling and retention of information by external service providers may differ from our own application's storage practices.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website and third-party security services may use cookies or similar technologies necessary for security, verification, and website operation.

You may control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain technologies may affect the operation of some website features.

8. Advertising

FreeAITools may display advertisements in the future, including advertisements provided by Google AdSense.

If advertising is enabled, advertising partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to deliver, personalize, measure, or manage advertisements, subject to applicable requirements and user choices.

Where legally required, we will provide appropriate privacy notices and consent options before enabling relevant advertising technologies.

You can learn more about Google's advertising-related data practices at Google's Advertising Privacy Information .

9. Data retention

Our AI endpoint is designed to return generated responses without instructing browsers or intermediaries to cache successful responses.

Daily rate-limit counters stored in Cloudflare KV are configured to expire after approximately 24 hours. Other usage counters, infrastructure logs, and information processed by third-party providers may be subject to different retention periods.

A no-store response header does not prevent Google or Cloudflare from retaining information under their own applicable policies.

10. International data processing

FreeAITools uses external infrastructure and AI providers. Information processed by these providers may be transferred to or processed in countries outside your country of residence, subject to applicable legal requirements and provider terms.

11. Privacy requests and your choices

If you have questions about our handling of information or want to make a privacy-related request, please contact us. We will review requests in accordance with applicable law and the information reasonably available to us.

Where information is controlled independently by Google, Cloudflare, or another third party, you may also need to contact that provider directly.
