Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Welcome to FreeAITools. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you visit our website and use our free AI, PDF, and image tools.

Website: https://freeaitools-5pd.pages.dev/

1. Information you provide

Public AI writing tools are currently unavailable. If these features are re-enabled, information you choose to submit, such as prompts, selected tools, and generation preferences, may be processed to provide the requested AI-generated results.

Please do not enter passwords, payment details, government identification numbers, confidential documents, sensitive personal information, or other information you do not want shared with an external AI provider.

2. AI processing and Google Gemini

Public AI text generation is currently suspended on FreeAITools. Our AI Writer, Caption Generator, Business Name Generator, and Email Writer are not currently available for public use.

If these AI features become available in the future, FreeAITools may use Google's Gemini API to process submitted prompts and generate responses. Before re-enabling these features, we will review the applicable provider requirements and update this Privacy Policy as necessary.

While public AI text generation is suspended, FreeAITools does not offer public AI prompt processing through its Gemini API integration.

If AI features are re-enabled, prompts submitted through those features may be transmitted from our Cloudflare-hosted backend to Google's Gemini API for processing and response generation. Such processing would be subject to the applicable provider terms and privacy practices.

Public AI text generation is currently suspended. If these features are re-enabled, the applicable Gemini API service tier and Google's then-current terms will determine how submitted prompts and generated responses are handled. Depending on the service terms, this may include processing for service improvement or human review. We will review and disclose relevant data practices before enabling public AI processing.

Please review Google's Gemini API Terms and Google's Privacy Policy for further details.

3. PDF and image tools

Our PDF and image tools are designed to process selected files locally in your web browser.

For these browser-based operations, the files you select are processed on your device rather than being intentionally uploaded to our Gemini API backend.

We recommend avoiding highly sensitive files when using any online service. Browser extensions and third-party software installed on your device may have their own data practices.

4. Security and usage limits

We use Cloudflare services to host and protect FreeAITools, support website security, and help distinguish legitimate requests from automated or abusive activity.

Public AI text generation is currently suspended. Security checks, request verification, and usage limits may apply to AI features if they are re-enabled.

Public AI generation is currently suspended, so the AI endpoint does not currently execute its IP-based rate limiting or Cloudflare KV usage-counter logic for public AI generation requests.

If AI generation is re-enabled, the backend is designed to read the request IP address and generate a SHA-256 hash using the date and IP address. This hash may be used as part of a temporary rate-limit key in Cloudflare KV. The configured expiration time for these counters is approximately 24 hours. The system also includes website-wide AI usage counters.

Hashing an IP address does not necessarily make the resulting information anonymous. We use this processing for service protection, abuse prevention, and fair resource allocation.

5. Cloudflare Turnstile

FreeAITools integrates Cloudflare Turnstile as a security verification measure for AI-related features. Public AI text generation is currently suspended, so AI generation requests do not currently proceed to backend Turnstile verification.

Turnstile scripts or components may still be present on website pages. Where loaded, Cloudflare may process technical information for security and verification purposes under its applicable privacy practices.

Learn more in Cloudflare's Privacy Policy .

FreeAITools is hosted using Cloudflare infrastructure. Cloudflare may process network and technical information necessary to deliver website content, maintain service performance, and protect against abuse.

The handling and retention of information by external service providers may differ from our own application's storage practices.

9. Data retention

Public AI text generation is currently suspended. If AI features are re-enabled, our AI endpoint is designed to return generated responses without instructing browsers or intermediaries to cache successful responses. This does not guarantee that external service providers will not retain information under their own policies.

Daily rate-limit counters stored in Cloudflare KV are configured to expire after approximately 24 hours. Other usage counters, infrastructure logs, and information processed by third-party providers may be subject to different retention periods.

A no-store response header does not prevent Google or Cloudflare from retaining information under their own applicable policies.
