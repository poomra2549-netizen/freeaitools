Merge PDF Files

Combine multiple PDF documents into one. Free, simple, and private.

    Maximum 25 MB per file and 100 MB combined. Large files may require more device memory.

    How to merge PDF files

    1. Select at least two PDF files.
    2. Use the arrow buttons to change their order.
    3. Click Merge PDF to download one combined file.

    Your privacy matters

    Your PDF documents are processed locally in your browser. The documents are not uploaded to our servers. An internet connection is needed to load the PDF processing library.

    Password-protected PDFs are not supported.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is this PDF merger free?

    Yes. You can merge PDF files for free without creating an account.

    Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

    No. PDF merging takes place locally in your browser. Your selected PDF documents are not uploaded to our servers for merging.

    Can I change the order of PDF files?

    Yes. After selecting your files, use the up and down arrow buttons to arrange them before merging.

    What are the file size limits?

    Each PDF can be up to 25 MB, with a combined selection limit of 100 MB. Larger files may require more device memory.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    Password-protected PDF documents are not supported. Please use PDF files that do not require a password to open.
