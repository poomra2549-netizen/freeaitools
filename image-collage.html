How to make a photo collage

  1. Select one or more JPG, PNG or WebP images.
  2. Choose a 2×2 or 3×3 grid.
  3. Reorder or remove images as needed.
  4. Adjust cell size, spacing and background color.
  5. Review the live collage preview.
  6. Download your finished collage as one image.

Two popular collage layouts

A 2×2 collage contains four square cells. A 3×3 collage contains nine square cells. You can leave some cells empty, where your chosen background color will appear.

How are images fitted?

Each selected image is center-cropped to fill its square cell. This prevents stretching, but may trim the edges of wide or tall photos. Image order can be adjusted before downloading.

Private browser-based collage maker

This tool uses your browser's Canvas API and does not send your selected image contents to FreeAITools servers for processing. No account or paid API key is required.

Important limitations

This version supports up to nine static images. Animated PNG and animated WebP are not supported. Original metadata, including EXIF and GPS information, may not be preserved.

The collage background is an opaque color, even when exporting to PNG or WebP. Transparent input images are drawn over that background.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Image Collage Maker free?

Yes. You can make collages without an account or payment.

Can I combine different image formats?

Yes. JPG, PNG and WebP images can be combined in the same collage.

How many images can I add?

You can add up to nine images in total.

Can I use only three images in a 2×2 grid?

Yes. The fourth cell will show your chosen background color.

Can I change image order?

Yes. Use the Move Up and Move Down buttons beside each selected image.

Are photos stretched?

No. Photos are center-cropped to fill the square cells while keeping their proportions.

Can I change the spaces between photos?

Yes. Adjust the Image spacing slider from 0 to 60 pixels.

Can I export JPG or WebP?

Yes, when supported by your browser. PNG is also available.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Collage creation happens locally in your browser.
